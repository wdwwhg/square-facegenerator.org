## Current tools

The public image converter, avatar maker, resizer, and pattern maker are free. Features marked as planned are not yet provided and are not a purchased or delivered service.

## Images and artwork

Upload only images you have permission to use. The tools do not change the rights attached to your source content. Original avatar parts and illustrations made for this workshop may be used in personal or commercial projects. This service does not run or reproduce the classic Flash Museum avatar tool.

## Patterns and dimensions

Patterns use generic RGB colors rather than manufacturer bead or thread codes. Match physical materials and check printed dimensions before crafting. Resizing enlarges existing pixels without inventing new detail.

## Keeping your work

The local tools do not provide cloud project saving. Download the results you want to keep.
