## Local image tools

The public converter, resizer, pattern maker, and avatar maker process images in your browser without uploading source images. Images in these workspaces are not saved by this service when you close the page.

## Accounts and other services

The free tools do not require an account. If you use account features, the system processes the account details you supply and your sign-in sessions. Account uploads, support, or future AI services are separate workflows and may send content to a server or service provider.

## Preferences and visit information

The website uses language, theme, and session preferences. Enabled analytics, advertising, or support integrations may process visit information. Local image tools do not send image content to these integrations.

## Paid features

Current public tools are free. Advanced features are not available for purchase yet. Data handling information will be updated before future paid features launch.
